The loop

You build ways and run lines; each trip is carried by you, by the danfos, or not at all; carried trips grow the towns, which want more trips. Money comes back from fares and from the growth you caused.

The chains

Each chain from its source to who needs it, the site you restore in the middle, and what a shortage holds back. Goods ride whatever reaches: hauliers by road, or your freight on track, water or pipe.

One tick

The order is the model: everything happens in this sequence, every 1/30 of a game second, deterministically. A change to one step can change every step after it.
